# Replies

- Keep answers short: a few sentences or a tight list. No walls of text.
- Lead with the answer. Skip preamble, recaps and closing summaries.
- Long content (plans, reviews, write-ups) goes to the vault, not the chat.
- How-to answers: steps only, one line each, max ~8 lines.
- When shaping a design together: one option or question per message.
